About Day of Caring

Over 3,000 students supported!

Serving Vanderburgh, Warrick, and Spencer Counties

The United Way of Southwestern Indiana Day of Caring is the largest coordinated volunteer service event in the region. Nearly a thousand volunteers from dozens of local companies and organizations come together to provide hands-on support to area non-profits.

Volunteers assist local organizations with landscaping, painting, outdoor clean-up, facility reorganization, computer setup, and interactive activities with youth and community members.
